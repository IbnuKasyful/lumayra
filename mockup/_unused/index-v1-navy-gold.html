Our specialism

Floor care is where Lumayra started

Floors are the largest finished surface in your building and the costliest to replace. We restore and maintain them with the right machine, pad and chemistry for each material.

Marble and stoneDiamond honing from 400 to 3000 grit, crystallisation, sealing
Vinyl and LVTStrip, seal and polish with slip-rated finishes
Concrete and epoxyRide-on scrubber-dryers, degreasing, line-marking safe methods
CarpetHot-water extraction and low-moisture encapsulation
